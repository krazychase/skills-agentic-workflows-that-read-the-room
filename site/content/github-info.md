### Awesome Copilot Workflows

Source: [Awesome Copilot workflows](https://awesome-copilot.github.com/workflows/) (redirects to the [github/awesome-copilot workflows folder](https://github.com/github/awesome-copilot/tree/main/workflows)). Ready-made agentic workflow examples include:

- `daily-issues-report`: a daily summary of repository issues.
- `weekly-comment-sync`: keeps comments in sync weekly.
- `ospo-org-health`, `ospo-stale-repos`, `ospo-contributors-report`, `ospo-release-compliance-checker`: open source program office health and compliance reports.
- `relevance-check` and `relevance-summary`: check and summarize relevance of contributions.
